About Emergency Dentistry

A dental emergency can be frightening and painful. Whether it's a knocked-out tooth, a severe infection, or a broken crown, prompt treatment is essential to save the tooth and alleviate pain. Nagu Dental is equipped to handle all types of urgent dental situations with priority scheduling.

Our team is trained to remain calm and provide quick, decisive care to resolve your emergency and get you back to comfort as soon as possible.

The Procedure

01

Rapid Triage

A quick assessment of the severity and cause of your dental emergency.

02

Pain Relief

Immediate measures to eliminate pain and stabilize the affected area.

03

Digital Diagnosis

Using high-speed imaging to see the internal damage and plan the repair.

04

Emergency Repair

The necessary treatment (extraction, root canal, or bonding) to fix the issue.

Common Emergencies

  • Severe toothache or swelling
  • Knocked-out or loose tooth
  • Chipped or broken tooth
  • Lost filling or crown
  • Injury to the jaw or gums
