About Pediatric Dentistry

At Nagu Dental, we love working with children! Our goal is to make every visit a positive, educational, and fun experience. We focus on preventive care to keep young smiles healthy and provide guidance on proper brushing and flossing habits that will last a lifetime.

From the first tooth to the teenage years, our specialized pediatric approach ensures that your child feels safe, comfortable, and excited about their dental health.

The Procedure

01

Orientation

A friendly introduction to the dental chair and tools to build trust and comfort.

02

Gentle Cleaning

Thorough yet careful removal of plaque, tailored for sensitive young mouths.

03

Sealants & Fluoride

Applying protective layers to help prevent cavities in deep grooves of the teeth.

04

Health Coaching

Engaging children and parents with tips for healthy eating and great oral habits.

Key Benefits

  • Prevents childhood dental anxiety
  • Promotes long-term oral health
  • Monitors dental development
  • Early detection of spacing issues
  • Fun and engaging environment
