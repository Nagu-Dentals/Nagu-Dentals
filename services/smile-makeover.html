About Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover is a customized plan that addresses all your dental concerns at once. Whether you have missing teeth, gaps, stains, or worn-down enamel, we design a sequence of treatments—including veneers, implants, whitening, and more—to achieve a balanced, radiant, and functional smile.

This is where art meets dentistry. Our specialists at Nagu Dental consider your skin tone, facial shape, and even your personality to create a smile that is uniquely yours.

The Procedure

01

Master Consultation

Identifying every concern and mapping out a multi-disciplinary treatment plan.

02

Functional Prep

Ensuring the foundation (gums and bone) is healthy before beginning aesthetic work.

03

Aesthetic Execution

Performing whitening, veneers, or orthodontic adjustments as per the design.

04

The Reveal

The final integration of all treatments for a cohesive, breathtaking transformation.
