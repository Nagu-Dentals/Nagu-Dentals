About Preventive Dentistry

At Nagu Dental, we believe that the best way to maintain a beautiful smile is to prevent issues before they start. Preventive dentistry is the foundation of our practice, focusing on regular maintenance and early detection to save you time, money, and discomfort in the long run.

Our approach combines advanced diagnostic tools with gentle, thorough cleaning techniques to ensure your oral health remains in peak condition.

The Procedure

01

Digital Exam

A comprehensive assessment of your teeth and gums using high-definition digital imaging.

02

Ultrasonic Scaling

Removal of plaque and tartar buildup using gentle ultrasonic technology.

03

Polishing

Buffing away surface stains to leave your teeth smooth and bright.

04

Fluoride Care

An optional strengthening treatment to protect enamel against future decay.

Key Benefits

  • Prevents cavities and decay
  • Reduces risk of gum disease
  • Early detection of oral issues
  • Long-term cost savings
  • Fresh breath and bright smile
