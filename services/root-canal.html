About Root Canal Treatment

A root canal is a treatment used to repair and save a tooth that is badly decayed or becomes infected. During a root canal procedure, the nerve and pulp are removed and the inside of the tooth is cleaned and sealed. Without treatment, the tissue surrounding the tooth will become infected and abscesses may form.

At Nagu Dental, we specialize in 'Single Visit Root Canals' using advanced rotary endodontics, making the process much faster and more comfortable than traditional methods.

The Procedure

01

Local Anesthesia

Ensuring the area is completely numb for a 100% painless experience.

02

Pulp Removal

Removing the infected pulp and cleaning the canals with microscopic precision.

03

Disinfection

Using specialized irrigants to eliminate bacteria within the complex canal system.

04

Sealing & Crown

Filling the canals and placing a crown to restore the tooth's strength and look.
