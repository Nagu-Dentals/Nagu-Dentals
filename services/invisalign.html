About Invisalign

Invisalign is the world's most advanced clear aligner system. Instead of traditional metal braces, Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, removable clear plastic trays to gradually shift your teeth into place. It's the ideal solution for adults and teens who want a straighter smile without the aesthetic impact of metal braces.

At Nagu Dental, we use the iTero Element® scanner to create a precise 3D digital scan of your teeth, allowing you to see your projected results before you even start treatment.

The Procedure

01

3D Digital Scan

A quick, comfortable scan of your teeth with no messy impressions.

02

Custom Plan

Mapping out the precise movements of your teeth and treatment duration.

03

Wear Your Aligners

Wear each set of aligners for about 1-2 weeks, removing them only to eat and clean.

04

Progress Checks

Quick visits every 6-8 weeks to ensure your treatment is on track.
