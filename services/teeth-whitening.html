About Teeth Whitening

Over time, factors like coffee, tea, wine, and aging can stain and dull your teeth. While over-the-counter options exist, professional teeth whitening at Nagu Dental offers a more powerful, even, and long-lasting result under the supervision of dental experts.

We use premium whitening agents that are designed to minimize sensitivity while maximizing brightness, giving you a radiant smile you'll be proud to show off.

The Procedure

01

Gingival Barrier

Applying a protective gel to your gums to prevent irritation from the whitening agent.

02

Gel Application

Applying a high-concentration professional whitening gel to the surface of your teeth.

03

Light Activation

Using a specialized LED light to accelerate the whitening process for faster results.

04

Final Rinse

Removing the gel to reveal your brighter, whiter smile in just about 60 minutes.
