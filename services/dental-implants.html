About Dental Implants

Losing a tooth can affect your confidence and your ability to eat and speak. Dental implants provide a permanent, stable solution that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants integrate with your jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.

At Nagu Dental, we use computer-guided implant surgery to ensure the highest level of precision and comfort for our patients.

The Procedure

01

3D Planning

Using CBCT scans to create a precise 3D map of your jaw and nerve locations.

02

Implant Placement

A minor surgical procedure to place the titanium post into the jawbone.

03

Healing Phase

A few months for the implant to fuse with the bone (osseointegration).

04

Crown Fitting

The attachment of a custom-crafted porcelain crown to complete your smile.

Key Benefits

  • Permanent tooth replacement
  • Restores full chewing power
  • Prevents jawbone deterioration
  • No slipping or discomfort
  • Protects adjacent healthy teeth
