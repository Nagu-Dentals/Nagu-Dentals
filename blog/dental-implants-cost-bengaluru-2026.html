Missing a tooth can affect far more than just your appearance. It impacts how you chew, speak, and feel about your smile. When it comes to replacing missing teeth, dental implants are widely recognized as the gold standard in modern restorative dentistry. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants mimic natural teeth by integrating directly into your jawbone to provide permanent, lifelike stability.

At Nagu Dental Clinic, backed by over 15+ years of clinical excellence, our team of oral surgeons and implantologists utilizes 3D digital imaging and computer-guided placement techniques to deliver predictable, painless implant treatments across our Bengaluru locations.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Bengaluru in 2026?

Understanding dental implant pricing helps patients make informed decisions about their oral health investment. In Bengaluru, the overall cost of a dental implant depends on several factors, including the implant brand, jawbone density, the custom crown material, and whether additional preparatory procedures (such as bone grafting or sinus lifts) are required.

Transparent Pricing at Nagu Dental

At Nagu Dental, a single premium titanium dental implant typically ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹45,000, depending on the brand and if bone grafting is required. We provide a detailed, no-hidden-costs treatment plan during your 3D scan consultation.

The Nagu Dental Implant Procedure: What to Expect

Our implant protocol is engineered for maximum patient comfort, safety, and long-term aesthetic perfection. Here is our 4-step treatment journey:

Step 1

3D Digital Scan & Consultation

We perform high-resolution CBCT 3D scanning to analyze your jawbone structure, nerve pathways, and sinus positions with millimeter precision.

Step 2

Custom Treatment Planning

Our specialists design a virtual 3D surgical guide tailored specifically to your anatomy, ensuring optimal position and aesthetic alignment.

Step 3

Painless Implant Placement

Using advanced local anesthesia and gentle rotary technology, the medical-grade titanium post is placed smoothly into the jawbone.

Step 4

Crown Attachment & Aftercare

Once osseointegration occurs, a custom porcelain or zirconia crown is securely fixed to fully restore your chewing power and radiant smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are dental implants painful?

A: No. The procedure is performed under advanced local anesthesia, making it virtually painless. Most patients report minimal discomfort post-procedure, easily managed with prescribed mild medication, and many return to daily activities the next day.

Q: How long do dental implants last?

A: With proper oral hygiene and routine dental checkups, titanium dental implants can last a lifetime, making them the most cost-effective long-term solution for missing teeth.
